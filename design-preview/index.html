About

Space traffic, factory floors, same problem.

A noisy sensor, a model that has to be right, and a machine that has to act in time.

I studied Electrical Engineering at UET Lahore on a full HEC merit scholarship, with a focus on embedded systems, FPGAs, AI and signal processing. My final year project became a year at KAUST CyberSaR with Prof. Ali Shoker, working on how satellites can detect and avoid collisions without waiting for the ground.

Before that I tracked geostationary satellites at SUPARCO, put a vision system on a live PepsiCo line, and built traffic violation detection for Punjab Safe Cities. I'm now looking for a PhD or MS position for 2027 in autonomy, embedded AI or trustworthy ML.

A camera rejects unknown objects on a conveyor, and the command to the STM32 travels over an encrypted, authenticated serial link. All of it runs on a Core i3 with no GPU.

Beyond Random Flips: Feature-Aware Label Poisoning for Targeted Attacks in Federated Learning

J. Saleem, U. Rashid, I. Javed, H. Anwaar

Submitted · IEEE ICASSP 2027

An attacker flips labels toward classes that already look similar, like cat to dog. The damage lands on chosen classes and looks like natural confusion, so it is harder to catch.

Errors concentrate on one target · flagged less often than random flips
